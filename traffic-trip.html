The map is a schematic (not-to-scale) graph of the real freeway network between UCSB and the Long Beach Yacht Club: 101, 1 (PCH), 23, 118, 126, 192, 2, 5, 10, 110, 405, 605, and 710. Each of the segments queries the Flow Segment Data endpoint at a hand-picked point that lies on that actual freeway, so speeds are the real thing. Leg minutes = real driving distance ÷ live speed. Responses are cached in localStorage for 10 minutes to be gentle on the trial key's daily quota. If a sample point snapped to the wrong road, the frc column below will show it (FRC0–1 = motorway, higher = smaller road).
